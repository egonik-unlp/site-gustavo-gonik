# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Spanish-speaking individuals (Argentina / Latin America) in three overlapping situations:

1. **Owners of artworks** who need a professional valuation: an inherited painting, a collection to insure or sell, a piece bought abroad. They arrive uncertain and slightly anxious; they need to trust the expert before they reveal what they own. This is the primary audience.
2. **Art-curious learners** considering courses on art appreciation, valuation, and the art market.
3. **Individuals decorating a home or office** who want expert guidance choosing and placing art rather than buying decoration blindly.

All three convert through a conversation, not a checkout: WhatsApp first (LatAm norm), then contact form / email, phone, and course-interest sign-up.

## Product Purpose

A single landing page presenting Gustavo Gonik, art expert. Appraisal (tasación) leads the narrative; courses and decoration consultancy follow as secondary offers. Success = a visitor trusts Gustavo's eye within seconds and starts a conversation through any of the contact channels. Hosted on Cloudflare Workers (Astro, static-first).

## Brand Personality

**Contemporary gallerist**: bold, current, design-forward. Authority through taste, not credentials-shouting. The page should feel like walking into a sharp contemporary gallery where the owner clearly knows more than you, and that's exactly why you came. Confident, visual, personal. Warm enough to invite the nervous inheritor; never clubby or cold.

Three words: **discerning, current, personal**.

## Anti-references

- **Generic corporate site**: blue-gray SaaS template, feature-card grids, stock handshakes.
- **Cold gallery minimalism**: white void, tiny gray text, unapproachable sparseness.
- **Loud marketing page**: urgency banners, aggressive CTAs, salesy countdown energy.
- Also avoid by implication: gold-on-parchment antique-dealer clichés. Contemporary, not dusty.

## Design Principles

1. **The art is the evidence.** Show artworks, not claims. Imagery of real art carries the authority; copy stays specific and brief.
2. **Authority through taste.** Design quality IS the credential. A page with a confident eye proves the expert has one.
3. **One expert, one voice.** This is a person, not an agency. Copy speaks as Gustavo; the page has a face.
4. **Every section opens a conversation.** Conversion is a WhatsApp message or a call, not a transaction. CTAs are invitations ("Contame qué tenés"), never pressure.
5. **Tasación leads, the rest follows.** Appraisal anchors the hierarchy; courses and consultancy are clearly present but secondary.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA baseline: body text ≥4.5:1 contrast, visible focus states, semantic landmarks, keyboard-complete navigation, `prefers-reduced-motion` alternatives for all animation. All copy in Spanish (`lang="es"`), useful alt text in the brand voice.
